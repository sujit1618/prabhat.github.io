NATURE 2027 DESIGN | PRABHAT CALENDARS

Open ../index.html in a browser to read the interactive story. This package contains one HTML file and this assets folder. CSS, JavaScript and the bilingual content are inside the HTML. Photos, recordings and fonts are local assets.

The top-right button switches between English and मराठी. English pages show English bird names first. Marathi pages show Marathi names first. Palanquin is used for Marathi and Philosopher for English.

Scroll through the 12 monthly chapters. June includes Alexandrine Parakeet and Ashy Prinia. Select The bird, Close-up or Habitat to change the photograph. The enlarge button opens the photograph in a dialog. Swipe horizontally across a photograph to change its view. Use the reading tabs for identification, habitat and food, or Looking for it. Expand Read a little more for the remaining detail.

Previous and next controls, left and right arrow keys and URL hashes navigate the story. Tabs and photo controls also support arrow keys. Escape closes the enlarged photograph. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Audio starts only through the player's controls, and playing another bird pauses the previous one.

Nine birds have local recordings. Four have links to the eBird audio catalogue. The story, local recordings, fonts and photos work offline. eBird and original-photo links open online. The complete English source content remains readable with JavaScript disabled.

Media sources, authors, licences and conversion details are recorded in media-credits.json and MEDIA-CREDITS.txt. Font licences are included. All photography comes from the existing Wikimedia-sourced collection, with no photographs extracted from the calendar PDFs.

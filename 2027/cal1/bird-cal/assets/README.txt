NATURE 2027 DESIGN | PRABHAT CALENDARS

Open ../index.html in a browser. All months appear consecutively in the approved monthly-page design. The persistent top row jumps to each month and follows your position while scrolling. On phones, swipe the month row horizontally to reach more months.

The top-right button switches between English and मराठी. Both bird names appear in the requested order in each language. Marathi uses Palanquin. June includes both Alexandrine Parakeet and Ashy Prinia. All photos, recordings and fonts are stored in this assets folder. The page works directly from local files. Original-photo links, eBird links and online audio catalogues open online.

NATURE 2027 DESIGN | PRABHAT CALENDARS

Unzip and open any month’s index.html directly in a browser. Each of the 12 month folders contains a self-contained index.html and an assets folder. Images, fonts and bundled recordings are embedded, so the page also works when copied on its own. No server, installation, CDN or external font service is needed.

The approved January design is used across all months, with Marathi names from the supplied calendar PDFs, fuller descriptions, natural habitat, food habits and distribution in India. The top-left heading, brown buttons, fine brown divider lines and mobile layout are shared across the collection. Month navigation, visible photo-credit blocks and the sources disclosure section have been removed. Each page ends with its own eBird species link.

Every month contains two additional supporting Wikimedia photographs in the description and habitat sections, alongside its main bird photograph. No photograph from the supplied PDFs is used. Habitat photographs illustrate the landscape type and do not confirm bird sightings at those locations.

Eight pages contain offline MP3 recordings: January, March, April, June, August, September, November and December. February, May, July and October link to the eBird audio catalogue instead. Online listening, original-photo links and eBird species links need internet.

Media credits and source records are retained in each assets folder and in media-manifest.json. CC BY photographs link to the source page containing the photographer and licence information. Required recording attribution remains beneath audio players. Audio files keep their CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Embedded fonts are supplied under the SIL Open Font License.

June is Alexandrine Parakeet. September is Common (Eurasian) Kestrel. December is Asian Green Bee-eater.

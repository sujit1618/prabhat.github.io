Coppersmith Barbet

About

The Coppersmith Barbet is a small, colorful bird known for its repetitive, metallic call. Found across India, it adds vibrant charm to urban parks, gardens, and wooded areas.

Identification

  • It has a green body with yellow streaks and a red forehead.
  • The throat is yellow with a red patch below it.
  • Eyes are dark with a bold black eye-ring.
  • Its bill is stout and black, suited for fruit pecking.
  • The call resembles a coppersmith striking metal, heard in rhythmic bursts.

Habitat & Behavior

Coppersmith Barbets prefer fig trees, groves, and city gardens. They feed on fruits and berries, nest in tree cavities, and sing persistently during the breeding season, often from exposed perches.

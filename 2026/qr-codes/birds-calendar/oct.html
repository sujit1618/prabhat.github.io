Golden Oriole

About

The Golden Oriole is a dazzling yellow songbird found across Europe and Asia. Its flute-like calls and swift, secretive movements make it a prized sight in leafy canopies.

Identification

  • Males are bright golden-yellow with black wings and tail.
  • Females are greenish-yellow with streaked underparts.
  • Both sexes have red eyes and a pinkish pointed bill.
  • Wings show a sharp contrast between black and yellow.
  • Its call is a rich, fluty whistle often heard at dawn.

Habitat & Behavior

Golden Orioles prefer deciduous forests, groves, and orchards. They forage for fruits and insects in treetops, build hanging nests, and migrate seasonally between breeding and wintering grounds.
